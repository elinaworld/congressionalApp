Welcome to the Congressional App Information Center!

This app is designed to help citizens learn about their government and participate in civic engagement. Here's what you need to know:

CONGRESSIONAL BASICS:
- The United States Congress is the legislative branch of the federal government
- It consists of two chambers: the House of Representatives and the Senate
- The House has 435 voting members, each representing a congressional district
- The Senate has 100 members, with 2 senators from each state

HOW LAWS ARE MADE:
1. A bill is introduced in either the House or Senate
2. It goes through committee review and debate
3. Both chambers must pass the same version
4. The President can sign it into law or veto it

YOUR RIGHTS AS A CITIZEN:
- Freedom of speech and expression
- Right to vote in elections
- Right to petition the government
- Right to peaceful assembly
- Right to contact your representatives

GETTING INVOLVED:
- Contact your representatives about issues you care about
- Attend town halls and public meetings
- Stay informed about current events
- Vote in all elections, not just presidential ones
- Join civic organizations and advocacy groups

Remember: Democracy works best when citizens are informed and engaged!
